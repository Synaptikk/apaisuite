How to use SparkFraud

What it does

SparkFraud identifies the most likely Spark / Express delivery driver who was at a specific register at a specific time — so an AP investigator can correlate a fraud event to a driver and that driver's order contents.

Workflow

  1. Set Store, Date, Event time, and the ± minute window (start narrow; widen if no candidates).
  2. Pick which delivery types to query — Spark, Express, Scheduled Grocery.
  3. Click Find candidates. Results stream in as Dispatcher returns trips, then OMS items follow per batch.
  4. Each trip card shows: confidence badge, driver, customer-delivery window, in-store shopping window (PICKED → DISPATCHED), and the orders they handled.
  5. Click a trip card to expand its item list (name, UPC, qty, price, status, customer first name).

Quick lookup

The search bar at the top auto-detects input type: order IDs (all-digit, comma-separated) go through OMS directly; a driver's name opens Dispatcher's native driver-search and brings the captured trip data back into the suite.

Watchlist (real-time monitoring)

Click + Watch on any trip card to add that driver to the watchlist. The extension polls Dispatcher in the background every 3 minutes via chrome.alarms. When a watched driver transitions into an active status — Enroute to Pickup, At Pickup, or Trip In Progress — you'll get an OS notification with their current order IDs. The order item details are auto-fetched and surfaced in the Watchlist panel's "Recent hits" section.

The poll runs as long as the extension is loaded — you don't need to keep the SparkFraud tab open. Drivers stay on the list until you click the × next to them. The hit-history ring buffer keeps the most recent 100 alerts.

Confidence levels

Each candidate trip carries an investigative lead quality label — VERIFIED, LIKELY, POSSIBLE, UNKNOWN, or CONFLICTING. These reflect how well the trip's data matches the register event you queried; they are NEVER proof and NEVER an accusation of guilt.

Limitations

  • Dispatcher's API caps responses at ~200 trips per query; very busy stores in wide time windows may exceed the cap.
  • Driver-name matching is loose (first + last word, case-insensitive) — middle names/initials are tolerated, but unusual punctuation may miss.
  • The watchlist needs a valid gscope session; if SSO has expired the poll silently no-ops until you sign back in.
