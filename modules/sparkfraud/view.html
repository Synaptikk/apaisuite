How to use SparkFraud

What it does

SparkFraud identifies the most likely Spark / Express delivery driver who was at a specific register at a specific time — so an AP investigator can correlate a fraud event to a driver and that driver's order contents.

Workflow

  1. Set Store, Date, Event time, and the ± minute window (start narrow; widen if no candidates).
  2. Click Find candidates.
  3. Each trip card shows a confidence badge, the driver, the customer's delivery window, when the driver was at the register, and their orders.
  4. Click a trip card to expand its item list (name, UPC, qty, price, status, customer first name).

Quick lookup

Type order IDs (comma-separated) or a driver's name in the search bar at the top.

Watchlist

Click + Watch on a trip to watch that driver. You'll get a desktop alert when they head to or arrive at the store. You don't need to keep this page open; remove a driver with ×.

Confidence levels

Each candidate trip carries an investigative lead quality label — VERIFIED, LIKELY, POSSIBLE, UNKNOWN, or CONFLICTING. These reflect how well the trip's data matches the register event you queried; they are NEVER proof and NEVER an accusation of guilt.
