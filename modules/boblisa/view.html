Back-to-back sales on the same register under 2 minutes apart are one customer paying twice and are left out. Money Center and Automotive second sales are hidden by default for the same reason.
A first transaction on a self-checkout or other unmanned register has no cashier to miss the item. Money Center pairs (62–63, 92–94: bill pay, debit loads, money orders) and Automotive (95: tires and service) are hidden by default because they are separate purchases, not merchandise a register missed.
