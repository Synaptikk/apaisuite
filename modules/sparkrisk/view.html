How SparkRisk Works

What the score means

Trips are ranked by how much longer they took than expected for their item count, and how they compare with the same driver’s earlier trips. A high score means “review this one first” — it is not proof of anything. Confirm every finding on video and in the order details.

Workflow

  1. Import an order file on the Import Data tab
  2. Work the Review Queue from the top
  3. Click a trip to see its timing, items and notes
  4. Record the outcome — cleared, confirmed or escalated — with notes

Privacy

Real order numbers and driver names are visible here and access is not logged — keep this data inside authorized systems.
