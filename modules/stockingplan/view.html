How the hours are worked out

  
Five separate calculations. The freight ones are CaseVisibility’s own — this module runs them, it doesn’t invent them.


  
1 · Freight hours

  
Straight from CaseVisibility’s calc_getStockingHoursByDept:

  
hours = count / CPH(dept)

  
    
    
  • CPH is per department — from the workforce-planning table when the store has one, otherwise CaseVisibility’s built-in list.
    • 
    
  • It is applied twice, once to full cases and once to inner packs, and the two are added:
      
    dept total = cases/CPH + inner packs/CPH
    • 
    
  • Vendor-stocked departments return 0 — not our labour.
    • 
    
  • Summed over every load CaseVisibility counts against this business date.
    • 
  

  
Store total = the seven area totals only. Departments break down their area, and D92/95 aisles break down two departments inside Food (Non-FDD) — so adding the levels together would count the same freight two or three times.

3 · If CaseVisibility’s figures are missing

  
Only when the per-department capture fails. The Labour card then reads our case rates instead of CaseVisibility estimate:

  
cases / 55   (food & consumables)
cases / 45   (GM)
+ inner packs / 80
rounded UP to the next half hour


  
4 · Labour hours

  
per associate = (scheduled minutes − scheduled meal) / 60

  
    
    
  • Team leads and coaches are listed but excluded from capacity — they run the shift, they don’t work freight.
    • 
    
  • Call-outs come out of working hours and are reported separately.
    • 
    
  • Stock 2 + Overnight is tonight’s stocking capacity. Stock 1 is tomorrow’s crew, because the overnight shift runs 10pm to 7am and a plan written today is finished by the morning after.
    • 
  


  
5 · The judgements

  

    
      
capacity ÷ freightunder 1.10 short, under 1.25 tight, otherwise OK

      
block hours ÷ its own hoursovernight above 0.78 hands a line to the morning; below 0.65 it takes D18 back

      
Stock 2 at or below 0.15GM is light — send them to help zone and pick grocery

      
Leftavailable − planned: what the shift still has for zoning, backroom and everything a plan doesn’t list

      
line ÷ its own median1.35 very heavy · 1.15 heavy · 0.80 no comment · 0.60 light · below that very light. Nothing under 2h gets an adjective.

    
  


  
6 · Rounding

  
Hours are held as whole minutes on capture and shown to one decimal. Drift across a full store day is under 0.1h.


  
Worth knowing

  
CaseVisibility charges an inner pack at the same rate as a full case. The plans this store actually sends don’t agree: the hours written for 3/19/67 land consistently about 45% of the way between the cases-only figure and the cases-plus-packs figure, on every date checked — the store works inner packs roughly twice as fast as CaseVisibility assumes.

  
This module quotes CaseVisibility’s number as it stands rather than applying a discount of its own. Read the breakpack-heavy departments — D19 crafts, D40 OTC, D46 beauty, D2 HBA, fashion — with that in mind.
