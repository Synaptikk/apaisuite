What associates said they found

Each alert closes with one of 12 fixed quick-select tags. Grouped into three families: nothing was there when the associate arrived, it was already cleaned up, or a hazard was confirmed and handled.

How the alert was answered

Separate from the tag: whether anyone accepted the alert at all. SafeIQ stores a name only on accepted alerts, so declining one is anonymous.

Cameras

One row per camera. Sort any column. The shaded column is the share of that camera's alerts closed as a non-issue: no_hazard_found, no_spill or no_object. Click a row to list those events with the associate and response time.

Associates

What each associate did with the alerts they accepted. Non-issue is how often they reported nothing there — the only refusal SafeIQ attributes to a person. vs expected divides that by what the store's own rate on the same cameras predicts, so working an area whose camera genuinely over-fires does not read as waving alerts away. Switch to All 12 tags for the exact tag each associate used. Click a row to see the frames they closed.

Who was on the clock

SafeIQ names only the person who accepted. The rest of the list is rebuilt from the routing: an alert goes first to the camera's responsible team (Store Systems camera list), but only to members who are clocked in (GTA punches; meal breaks don't count). After 3 minutes, or once they all tap Not Available, it goes to every team lead and coach on shift. Let pass counts alerts that went past 3 minutes, or were never accepted, while the associate was on the clock for that team. Click a row to see those alerts.
